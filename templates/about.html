About Recipe Finder

Cook more of what you love, with less planning effort.

What it does

Recipe Finder & Meal Planner helps you find recipes, save favorites, plan a week of meals and turn that plan into a shopping list. Search works across recipe names, ingredients, cuisines, categories and diets, and filters can be combined.

Built with

Python and Django on the back end, with Bootstrap 5 on the front end. The project uses Django's own authentication, forms, admin and ORM, and works with SQLite for development or PostgreSQL for production.

A note on the sample data

The bundled recipes and nutrition values are demo content created for this project. Nutrition numbers are rough estimates and should not be used for medical or dietary decisions.
